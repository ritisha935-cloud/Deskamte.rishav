Help

Who does the work?

Claude, built right into this page. Each team’s helper is told what that team does, so the writing fits the job.

Do I need an account or a key?

No keys are needed. The first time you run a task, you’ll be asked once to allow it.

Is my work saved?

Tap Save on any result to keep it in the Saved tab. Your Saved items and your last five task titles per team stay in this browser on this device.

Can I change a result?

Yes. After a result appears, tap a quick change such as Shorter, or type your own, then tap Update it.

Ready to get started?

Pick a team and give it your first task. The finished work comes back in moments.
